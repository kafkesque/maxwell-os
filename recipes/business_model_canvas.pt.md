ZONE 2 - BODY

🔥 TRIGGER
  When a solopreneur has a business idea that needs structured modeling, or when an existing business model requires systematic evaluation against market realities and FB-grounded principles.

🔧 PREREQUISITE
  Business idea articulated in one sentence. Target market identified (can be fuzzy — PT will sharpen it). Access to `tools/mcp_fb_server.py` with loaded entrepreneurship + business index. 60-90 minutes of focused attention. Honesty about what you don't know (unknowns are data, not failure).

📋 PHASES

  Phase 1: Customer Segments + Value Propositions

    Step 1 [Required] Identify 1-3 primary customer segments. For each: describe their job-to-be-done, pain points, and current alternatives.
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "customer segmentation solopreneur market"
      → Gate: At least 1 segment defined with job/pain/alternative triplet

    Step 2 [Required] Articulate the value proposition for each segment. What specific problem does the product solve? Why would they switch from current alternatives?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "value proposition design unique selling point"
      → Gate: Value prop stated in one sentence per segment. No jargon. A 12-year-old would understand it.

    🚧 GATE → Phase 2
      Decision Matrix:

      │ FB grounding validates approach          │  ✅  │      │
      ├──────────────────────────────────────────┼──────┼──────┤
      │ Overall                                  │ PASS │      │
      └──────────────────────────────────────────┴──────┴──────┘

  Phase 2: Channels + Customer Relationships + Revenue Streams

    Step 3 [Required] Map how customers discover, evaluate, purchase, receive, and get support for the product. For solopreneurs: which channels are OWNED vs RENTED?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "distribution channel strategy owned vs rented platforms"
      → Gate: At least one owned channel identified (email list, website, direct outreach)

    Step 4 [Required] Define the customer relationship type: personal assistance, self-service, automated, community, or co-creation.
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "customer relationship model solopreneur"
      → Gate: Relationship type selected with rationale

    Step 5 [Required] Identify revenue streams: one-time, subscription, usage-based, freemium, or hybrid. What's the unit of value customers pay for?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "revenue model pricing strategy solopreneur"
      → Gate: Revenue model stated with unit price and forecast (even if rough)

    🚧 GATE → Phase 3
      Decision Matrix:
      ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┬──────┬──────┐
      │ Criterion                                │ Pass │ Fail │
      ├──────────────────────────────────────────┼──────┼──────┤
      │ ≥1 owned channel identified              │  ✅  │      │
      │ Relationship type selected + rationale   │  ✅  │      │
      │ Revenue model with unit price defined    │  ✅  │      │
      │ FB validation on all 3 dimensions        │  ✅  │      │
      ├──────────────────────────────────────────┼──────┼──────┤
      │ Overall                                  │ PASS │      │
      └──────────────────────────────────────────┴──────┴──────┘

  Phase 3: Key Resources + Activities + Partnerships

    Step 6 [Required] List the key resources needed: physical, intellectual, human, and financial. Which are OWNED (unfair advantage) vs ACCESSED?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "key resources competitive advantage solopreneur"
      → Gate: Resource list with owned/accessed distinction

    Step 7 [Required] Define key activities: what must you do daily/weekly/monthly to deliver the value proposition? What activities can be SYSTEMATIZED (PT candidates)?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "solopreneur productivity systems business operations"
      → Gate: Activities listed with systemize vs manual flag

    Step 8 [Required] Identify key partners and suppliers. What do you OUTSOURCE that others do better? What partnerships reduce risk or acquire resources?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "strategic partnership leverage solopreneur"
      → Gate: Partners listed with what they provide and why not in-house

    🚧 GATE → Phase 4
      Decision Matrix:
      ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┬──────┬──────┐
      │ Criterion                                │ Pass │ Fail │
      ├──────────────────────────────────────────┼──────┼──────┤
      │ Resources with owned/accessed flags      │  ✅  │      │
      │ Activities with systemize/manual flags   │  ✅  │      │
      │ Partners rationale documented            │  ✅  │      │
      │ FB grounding validates all 3             │  ✅  │      │
      ├──────────────────────────────────────────┼──────┼──────┤
      │ Overall                                  │ PASS │      │
      └──────────────────────────────────────────┴──────┴──────┘

  Phase 4: Cost Structure + Viability Check

    Step 9 [Required] List all costs: fixed (rent, subscriptions, software), variable (per-unit costs), and one-time (setup, equipment). Calculate monthly burn rate.
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "cost structure lean startup burn rate solopreneur"
      → Gate: Costs categorized. Monthly burn rate calculated. Break-even point estimated.

    Step 10 [Required] Viability check: Does the revenue model cover costs at realistic volume? What's the minimum viable customer count? What's the margin per customer?
      → Grounded in: FB retrieved by intent "business viability validation breakeven analysis"
      → Gate: Break-even customer count calculated. Margin per customer > 0.
